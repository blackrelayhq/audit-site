Who we are

Black Relay (website and dApp front-end audits) is operated by Esa. The operator's full legal name and postal address will be added here before any outreach. Contact: esa@mail.grokbot.com.

What we collect and why

  • Business contacts we email. We collect a project's publicly listed business email address, the page we found it on, the project name, the date, and our notes about the public website. We use these to send one email about the website. Legal basis: legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) in offering relevant B2B services to the published business contact.
  • Clients and enquiries. We collect your email, the URLs you ask us to audit, our correspondence, and payment details (the transaction hash and sending wallet address). We use these to deliver the audit, answer questions and keep accounting records. Legal basis: contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) and legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • This website. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. We set no cookies and run no analytics. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Website checks

We only look at public pages and the response headers that any browser receives. We don't log in, probe for hidden files, attempt attacks, or ask for keys, seed phrases or admin access.

How long we keep data

Lead data: up to 6 months after our last contact, or until you object. If you object, we keep only your address on a suppression list so we never email you again. Client records: as long as accounting law requires.

Sharing

We don't sell data. Our processors are our email provider and GitHub (hosting). Payments are made on public blockchains, which are public by nature.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction or deletion, object to processing (including any marketing) at any time, and complain to your data-protection authority. Email esa@mail.grokbot.com or simply reply "no" to our email.
