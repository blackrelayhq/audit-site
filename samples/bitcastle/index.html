Findings

1. The homepage takes about a minute to finish loading on mobileSpeed

In a Lighthouse mobile run, the first content appeared at 21.8 s and the main content at 59.2 s. The page downloads about 10.5 MB, including a 2.1 MB main app script, and roughly 2.4 MB of JavaScript goes unused. Desktop LCP was 17.2 s.

Fix: Split the Nuxt bundle per route, defer third-party scripts, and server-render the hero so it appears before the app boots.

2. No security headers on the homepageWeb hygiene

The /en response has no Strict-Transport-Security, Content-Security-Policy, X-Frame-Options or X-Content-Type-Options header. On an exchange login and sign-up page, missing frame protection is a clickjacking risk.

Fix: Add HSTS, X-Frame-Options: DENY (or CSP frame-ancestors 'none') and nosniff at the CDN or load balancer.

3. The headline is a promo line, not a value propositionUX / conversion

"New campaign every day!" doesn't tell a new visitor this is an exchange, or why to pick it (screenshot item 1).

Fix: Lead with what bitcastle is and its main advantage (for example its fees), and keep the campaign as a secondary banner.

4. Typo on a key trust statTrust signals

The user count reads "1 millions +" (screenshot item 3). Small errors on headline numbers make visitors doubt the numbers themselves.

Fix: Change it to "1 million+".

5. Console errors and failed requests on loadQuality

Lighthouse logged failed resource loads (404) and a "Library execution failed" script error on the homepage.

Fix: Remove or fix the broken resource URLs and the failing third-party library.

6. Unlabelled buttons, links and imagesAccessibility

Lighthouse flags icon-only buttons with no name, social links (Facebook, Twitter) with no readable text, images without alt text, and low-contrast text.

Fix: Add aria-labels to icon buttons and links, alt text to images, and raise the contrast of small text.

7. No robots.txtSEO

/robots.txt returns 404. A sitemap.xml exists, but crawlers aren't pointed to it.

Fix: Publish a robots.txt containing a Sitemap: line.
