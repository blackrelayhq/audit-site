What we check

Five areas that decide whether a visitor connects a wallet, trusts your project — or bounces.

UX & conversion

Connect-wallet entry point, CTA clarity, mobile layout, and what a first-time visitor sees above the fold.

SEO & AI-search visibility

Titles, meta descriptions, canonical, sitemap/robots, structured data, and machine-readability for AI answer engines (crawlable text, llms.txt).

Speed & Core Web Vitals

LCP, CLS and blocking time from Lighthouse lab runs, page weight and unused JavaScript — because slow front-ends read as risky front-ends.

Security headers & web hygiene

HSTS, CSP, clickjacking protection, nosniff, referrer policy, mixed content and security.txt — checked passively from the public response headers.

Trust signals

Team visibility, links to contract audits, docs, contact paths and link hygiene — the difference between "credible" and "looks like a rug".

FAQ

No. Everything in the audit is done from the outside, the same way a real visitor — or attacker — sees your site. We never ask for private keys, seed phrases, or admin access. Fixes in the Audit + Fix Plan are written from what we can see publicly; no repo access needed.

What's in the free 3-finding preview?

Email us your URL and we'll send back three real findings from your site — usually the highest-impact ones. It's free, involves no payment, and lets you judge our quality before committing to a paid tier.

Which tier should I pick?
